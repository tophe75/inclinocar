Place your InclinoCar_app_icon.png here renamed to icon.png

Also copy it to each mipmap folder as ic_launcher.png at these sizes:
  mipmap-mdpi/ic_launcher.png      — 48x48
  mipmap-hdpi/ic_launcher.png      — 72x72
  mipmap-xhdpi/ic_launcher.png     — 96x96
  mipmap-xxhdpi/ic_launcher.png    — 144x144
  mipmap-xxxhdpi/ic_launcher.png   — 192x192

You can resize with any image editor or use https://icon.kitchen
